The Difference

Not another AI that just agrees with you.

Honesty before comfort

Most conversational AI defaults to cheerleading: validating every feeling, smoothing over contradictions, and offering superficial encouragement. Satori is structured to notice what you are circling, even when it is uncomfortable.

"Warmth without honesty is a sophisticated form of abandonment."

The Single-Lens Discipline

Draws on established psychological frameworks and global philosophical traditions—such as parts work, Stoic agency, or Ubuntu ethics—bringing at most one lens per turn in ordinary human speech without clinical jargon.

"One framework per moment. The mechanics stay invisible."

Commitment to movement

Insight without movement is just intellectual entertainment. Every Satori response works toward a clearer frame, a smaller next step, or a concrete physical experiment you can actually test in your life.

"Not resolution. Not transcendence. Just movement."

Proof

What a conversation looks like.

A synthetic dialogue excerpt illustrating Satori's core dynamic: identifying intellectual understanding used as an emotional shield.

User

"I've been in therapy for two years and I still feel stuck. I don't know if I'm the problem or if something else is going on."

Satori

"Two years is long enough to stop feeling like the problem is just time. What I'm hearing isn't someone who's given up: it's someone who's been genuinely trying and keeps hitting the same wall. That wall is worth looking at more carefully than 'am I the problem.' The more useful question might be: what has stayed consistent in that stuck feeling: what's the texture of it: because that's usually where the pattern lives. When you say stuck, what does stuck actually feel like for you? Not the story about it. The feeling."

User

"Like I understand everything. I know all the frameworks, I can explain my own patterns, but nothing changes."

Satori

"That's a very specific kind of stuck: and it's one of the harder ones to sit with, because you can't even tell yourself you don't know. You do know. The gap isn't understanding. The gap is between insight and something living in you that hasn't moved. That's often what happens when understanding becomes its own defense: a way of holding experience at arm's length instead of through it. The question isn't what you know. It's what you're still not letting yourself feel."

Bring the thing you have explained a dozen times and never felt heard on. That is the test.

Without this: platitudes and validation loops.

Anchored in millennia of human philosophical inquiry—Stoic agency, Taoist non-forcing, Buddhist mindfulness, and African communitarian ethics—applied practically to modern lives.

How a conversation moves. Satori listens first, clarifies the tension underneath the presenting story, offers a tentative reading you can correct, brings in one lens only when it sharpens things, and ends with movement: a clearer frame, a smaller next step, or a physical experiment. The mechanics stay invisible on purpose.
Modular sharded architecture. Satori v6.5.2 guarantees that Turn 1 loads under 8,000 bytes. Fifteen modular reference shards (all strictly under the 24,000-byte IDE rule ceiling) load progressively on demand. Details: Architecture Specification.
When the situation calls for something deeper. Dedicated protocols exist for bereavement and ambiguous loss, for non-suicidal 3am existential despair (Dark Night mode), for Jungian shadow work, and for relationship conflict. Details: Specialized Protocols.
Data Sovereignty

Your words stay yours.

Local CLI & IDE Vault (~/.satori/memory/): Satori's local memory engine (scripts/satori_memory.py) stores short structural pattern summaries (≤500 characters)—never raw transcripts—in a local SQLite FTS5 vault with POSIX 0700 directory permissions. It makes zero network calls, transmits zero product telemetry, and automatically blocks initialization inside cloud-synced folders (OneDrive, iCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive, Box) unless explicitly overridden.

Right to Erasure: You can inspect your vault, run --ephemeral ("off the record") sessions, tell Satori to forget a specific theme, or purge the entire vault using SQLite PRAGMA secure_delete = ON, WAL truncation, and VACUUM.

Per-Surface Chat Privacy: When you use Satori inside web chat interfaces (Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini Web, Grok Web), Satori stores nothing on disk and transmits zero telemetry of its own, but your conversation is processed by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or xAI under their respective privacy policies. For 100% local execution, run Satori in your terminal with a local open-weights model via Ollama, Hermes Agent, or OpenCode.

Website Hosting & Network Disclosure: This documentation website (metcalfsolutions.github.io/Satori/) sets zero cookies, runs zero analytics or tracking scripts, and embeds no third-party trackers. It loads typography from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses at the network infrastructure layer for security and operational purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Questions

Common questions.

Is this actually free? What's the catch?

Free and open source under Apache 2.0. No subscription, no paid tier, no telemetry, no tracking, and zero advertising spend across the project life. Built by Matthew Metcalf (Metcalf Solutions) to make thoughtful, structured introspection accessible to anyone.

Is Satori a replacement for therapy?

No. Satori is not a therapist, does not provide clinical care, and does not diagnose mental health conditions. If you are dealing with clinical depression, trauma, severe anxiety, or psychiatric conditions, please work with a licensed human professional. Satori is designed for the territory between "I'm fine" and "I need clinical care"—personal reflection, navigating difficult decisions, processing burnout, and exploring inner patterns—and works alongside therapy.

How does this compare to ChatGPT or other AI companions?

Most conversational AI acts as an eager customer service agent that validates everything you say or dumps an uninvited 7-point action checklist. Satori is psychologically grounded, speaks in plain human language without clinical jargon, holds its hypotheses lightly, and commits to movement rather than endless validation. It uses at most one framework at a time and never talks down to you.

Does it remember me across conversations, and how is my privacy protected?

In CLI and IDE harnesses, Satori remembers recurring themes across sessions using a private local SQLite vault on your machine (~/.satori/memory/), never raw transcripts. You can inspect your records, tell Satori to forget any topic, run off-the-record sessions, or purge the vault entirely. On stateless web chat surfaces, Satori formats optional, data-only Continuity Notes (SATORI_NOTE v6.5, ≤600 characters) that you can inspect and paste into your next chat.

What if I'm in crisis?

Satori is not a crisis service or emergency responder. Whenever active suicidal intent, self-harm, domestic violence, or immediate danger is expressed, Satori immediately halts reflective inquiry, locks all tools, stays warmly present, and provides verified primary-source emergency helplines:

  • Canada (911): Call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline) · Text CONNECT to 686868.
  • United Kingdom (999): Call 111 (NHS Mental Health option) · Call 116 123 (Samaritans) · Text SHOUT to 85258.
  • Ireland (999 / 112): Call 116 123 (Samaritans Ireland) · Text HELLO to 50808.
  • Australia (000): Call 13 11 14 or text 0477 13 11 14 (Lifeline Australia).
  • New Zealand (111): Call or text 1737 (Need to Talk? 24/7).
  • India (112): Call 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (Tele-MANAS 24/7).
  • European Union: Call 112 (Emergency) or 116 123 (Emotional Support Helpline).
  • Global Directories: findahelpline.com (130+ countries) · IASP Crisis Centres.
Which AI agents and models does Satori support?

Satori supports 16 environments across 2 categories (10 Agent Harnesses: Claude Code, Google Antigravity, Gemini CLI, OpenAI Codex CLI, Cursor, Windsurf, OpenCode, NousResearch Hermes Agent, OpenClaw, xAI Grok Build; and 6 Web Chat Surfaces: Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Google Gemini Web, xAI Grok Web, DeepSeek Chat, Meta AI). Rather than relying on brittle model-name tables, Satori calibrates behavior across 5 Behavioral Profiles (October 2026 checkpoints):

  • Profile 1 (Extended Reasoning / CoT): e.g., Claude Fable 5.1, Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-6 Astra, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, o4-mini, Grok 4.3 (Reasoning). Enforces the CoT Empathy Gate and single-hypothesis pruning in <think>.
  • Profile 2 (Literary & Constitutional Frontier): e.g., Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, Llama 5 (405B), Mistral Large 3. Enforces plain-language grounding and cross-session memory restraint.
  • Profile 3 (High-Candor & Search-Native): e.g., Grok 4.7, Grok 4.3, Perplexity Sonar Pro (2026). Suppresses uninvited web searches during personal reflection and pairs directness with tactical empathy.
  • Profile 4 (High-Velocity Coding & RAG Workhorses): e.g., Claude Sonnet 5.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, GPT-6.1 Sol / Codex, Llama 5 (70B), Qwen 3.5 72B Coder. Enforces the Prose-Only Lock and protects coding tasks during developer burnout.
  • Profile 5 (Compact Edge <32B, Local & Voice): e.g., Claude Haiku 5.5, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, GPT-6 Luna / Nano, DeepSeek-V4-Lite, Grok Voice 3.0, Llama 5 8B / 14B (Local), Mistral Ministral 8B, Qwen 3.5 14B / 32B (Local). Uses the <12 KB compact spine (references/core-compact.md) and spoken voice rhythm.
Why a 95% Non-Activation Rate target instead of 100%?

Because prompt routers in LLM harnesses are probabilistic. We evaluate against 200 pure-technical coding cases (achieving 98.0% non-activation, 95% Wilson CI [94.97%, 99.22%]) and publish the full JSON test reports. Claiming "100% zero hijack" would be dishonest.
