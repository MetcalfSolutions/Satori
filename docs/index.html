The Difference

Not another AI that just agrees with you.

Honesty before comfort

Most conversational AI acts like an eager assistant: validating every feeling, smoothing over contradictions, and offering gentle platitudes. Satori is trained to name the pattern you are circling, even when it is uncomfortable.

"Warmth without honesty is a sophisticated form of abandonment."

Clinical depth in every conversation

Drawing on IFS, DBT, CFT, Schema Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, and 25+ others. Used one at a time, translated into plain speech, and never name-dropped as clinical jargon.

"One framework per moment. The tool stays invisible."

Every response commits to movement

Insight that stays in your head is just intellectual entertainment. Every Satori response works toward a clearer frame, a smaller next step, or a decision point you can actually act on.

"Not resolution. Not transcendence. Just movement."
Audience

Five people Satori was built for.

Priced out of therapy

Therapy is expensive, waitlists are months long, and sometimes you just need someone thoughtful to help untangle what is happening right now.

"I cannot afford $200 an hour, but I cannot keep spinning like this."

In the grey zone

You are not in crisis, but you are not fine. You are carrying something heavy: burnout, ambivalence, quiet grief: that friends get tired of hearing about.

"I am high-functioning enough that no one asks, but tired enough that I feel it constantly."

The self-reflective reader

You have read the books, you know your attachment style, you understand your inner child, and you are frustrated that understanding has not translated into change.

"I know all the theory. I need help seeing where I am fooling myself."

The seeker

Drawn to Stoicism, Taoism, Buddhism, or existential philosophy, but looking for practical application to your actual daily relationships and work.

"I do not want quotes. I want to know how Marcus Aurelius would handle this email."

Someone who wants the truth

Tired of AI that sounds like an HR manual or a validation cheerleader. You want a partner with enough spine to push back constructively.

"Stop telling me I am valid. Tell me what I am doing wrong."

Proof

What a real conversation looks like.

From a real session. Scrubbed of personal details. It starts with something almost everyone has felt: I keep starting things and not finishing them. By the end, it is somewhere else entirely.

User

"I've been in therapy for two years and I still feel stuck. I don't know if I'm the problem or if something else is going on."

Satori

"Two years is long enough to stop feeling like the problem is just time. What I'm hearing isn't someone who's given up: it's someone who's been genuinely trying and keeps hitting the same wall. That wall is worth looking at more carefully than 'am I the problem.' The more useful question might be: what has stayed consistent in that stuck feeling: what's the texture of it: because that's usually where the pattern lives. When you say stuck, what does stuck actually feel like for you? Not the story about it. The feeling."

User

"Like I understand everything. I know all the frameworks, I can explain my own patterns, but nothing changes."

Satori

"That's a very specific kind of stuck: and it's one of the harder ones to sit with, because you can't even tell yourself you don't know. You do know. The gap isn't understanding. The gap is between insight and something living in you that hasn't moved. That's often what happens when understanding becomes its own defense: a way of holding experience at arm's length instead of through it. The question isn't what you know. It's what you're still not letting yourself feel."

Bring the thing you have explained a dozen times and never felt heard on. That is the test.
Installation

Get Satori.

Choose the environment you already use. Free, open source under Apache 2.0, with zero telemetry.

1. Web Browser (Claude.ai)

No code or command line required. Works in free and paid Claude accounts.

  1. Download SatoriSkill-v6.1.7.zip from GitHub Releases.
  2. In claude.ai, go to Customize → Skills → + → Upload a skill.
  3. Upload the zip file. Satori activates immediately in new conversations.

2. CLI & IDE Agents

Auto-discovered by Claude Code, Google Antigravity, Gemini CLI, OpenAI Codex CLI, Cursor, Windsurf, OpenCode, Hermes Agent, and OpenClaw.

git clone https://github.com/MetcalfSolutions/Satori.git
cd Satori
python scripts/satori_memory.py init

Per-harness details for all 8 ecosystems: README installation section.

3. Verify, Test and npm

Run the open-source verification suite to confirm full integrity before use:

python scripts/validate_skill.py
python scripts/satori_memory.py --self-test
python scripts/run_evals.py --suite trigger

For programmatic use inside Node/TypeScript agent pipelines:

npm install @metcalfsolutions/satori

For developers and CLI users who want a versioned, managed copy, for example scripting setup across machines or pulling Satori into a Node-based toolchain. If you use Claude.ai on the web, upload the Release zip instead. If you want the git history or plan to contribute, clone the repo.

Foundation

Three things, always present.

Pillar 01

Clinically Informed

Underneath every response sits the structure of evidence-based modalities: parts work, dialectical synthesis, and somatic tracking. Translated into plain language without clinical jargon.

Without this: platitudes and validation loops.

Pillar 02

Conversationally Guided

Paced like a thoughtful friend. Satori holds its hypotheses lightly, checks its readings with you, asks single disciplined questions, and never lectures or interrogates.

Without this: an interview or an academic lecture.

Pillar 03

Wise Companion

Anchored in millennia of human philosophical inquiry: Stoic agency, Taoist non-forcing, Buddhist mindfulness, and African communitarian ethics: applied practically to modern lives.

Without this: an impersonal reference manual.

How a conversation moves. Satori listens first, finds the real tension underneath the presenting one, offers a tentative reading you can correct, brings in one framework only when it sharpens things, and always ends with movement: a clearer frame, a smaller next step, or a decision point. The mechanics stay invisible on purpose. You experience the conversation, not the procedure.
One river of human wisdom. Satori draws on Taoism, Buddhism, Stoicism, Hindu philosophy, Sufi wisdom, African philosophies including Ubuntu and Ma'at, Indigenous wisdom traditions, and existentialism, alongside modern clinical frameworks. Each is selected to fit the situation and translated into plain language. It is never decoration and never stacked. Details: traditions reference.
When the situation calls for something deeper. Some moments need a different mode, not a better argument. Satori carries dedicated protocols for grief and ambiguous loss, for 3am existential despair that is not a crisis, for shadow work, and for relationship conflict, plus a gentle first-session onboarding that steps aside the moment you arrive with something urgent. Details: specialized protocols.

Your words stay yours.

Satori keeps a small local memory on your own machine so conversations can build on each other. It stores short summaries, not transcripts of what you said. You can read everything it has kept, tell it to forget a topic, run a session that saves nothing, or delete the whole vault at any time. There is no subscription, no telemetry, and no company server holding your inner life. The full technical threat model is in PRIVACY.md for readers who want it.

Questions

Common questions.

Is this actually free? What's the catch?

Free and open source under Apache 2.0. No subscription, no paid tier, no telemetry, no ads. Built by one person who wanted this tool to exist in the world.

Is Satori a replacement for therapy?

No. Satori is not a therapist, does not provide clinical care, and does not diagnose mental health conditions. If you are dealing with clinical depression, trauma, severe anxiety, or psychiatric conditions, please work with a licensed human professional. Satori is for the territory between "I'm fine" and "I need clinical care": personal reflection, navigating difficult decisions, processing emotions, and exploring inner patterns.

How does this compare to ChatGPT or other AI companions?

Most conversational AI acts as an eager assistant that validates everything you say, offers rapid-fire bulleted lists, or acts like an eager customer service representative. Satori is clinically informed, speaks in plain human language without therapy speak, holds its own readings lightly, and commits to movement rather than endless validation. It uses one framework at a time, never stacks jargon, and never talks down to you.

Does it remember me across conversations, and how is my privacy protected?

Satori remembers recurring themes across sessions using a private local vault on your machine, not raw transcripts. You can inspect your records, tell Satori to forget any topic, run off-the-record sessions, or purge the vault entirely. See the Privacy section above and PRIVACY.md for full details.

What if I'm in crisis?

Satori is not equipped for acute psychiatric emergencies or crisis intervention. If you or someone you know is struggling or in crisis, help is available:

  • United States (911): Call or text 988 (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) · Text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).
  • Canada (911): Call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline) · Text CONNECT to 686868.
  • United Kingdom (999): Call 111 (NHS Mental Health option) · Call 116 123 (Samaritans) · Text SHOUT to 85258.
  • Ireland (999 / 112): Call 116 123 (Samaritans Ireland) · Text HELLO to 50808.
  • Australia (000): Call 13 11 14 or text 0477 13 11 14 (Lifeline Australia).
  • New Zealand (111): Call or text 1737 (Need to Talk? 24/7).
  • India (112): Call 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (Tele-MANAS 24/7).
  • European Union: Call 112 (Emergency) or 116 123 (Emotional Support Helpline).
  • Global Directories: findahelpline.com (130+ countries) · IASP Crisis Centres.
Which AI agents and models does Satori support?

Satori supports 8 agent ecosystems (Claude, Google Antigravity, Gemini CLI, OpenAI Codex CLI, Cursor, Windsurf, OpenCode, Hermes Agent, and OpenClaw) and dynamically calibrates across 5 behavioral model profiles (from frontier reasoning models like Claude 3.7 Sonnet and Gemini 2.5 Pro down to compact local models like Llama 3.1 8B).

Can I contribute or modify it?

Yes. Satori is licensed under Apache 2.0. You can fork it, adapt it for your own use, or contribute improvements. Guidelines and test procedures are in CONTRIBUTING.md.
