The Difference

Not another AI that just agrees with you.

Honesty before comfort

Most conversational AI defaults to cheerleading: validating every feeling, smoothing over contradictions, and offering superficial encouragement. Satori is structured to notice what you are circling, even when it is uncomfortable.

"Warmth without honesty is a sophisticated form of abandonment."

The Single-Lens Discipline

Drawing on IFS, DBT, CFT, Schema Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, Stoicism, and Ubuntu. Used at most one framework per moment, translated into plain human speech, and never stacked or name-dropped as jargon.

"One framework per moment. The tool stays invisible."

Commitment to movement

Insight without movement is just intellectual entertainment. Every Satori response works toward a clearer frame, a smaller next step, or a concrete physical experiment you can actually act on.

"Not resolution. Not transcendence. Just movement."
Audience

Five people Satori was built for.

Priced out of therapy

Therapy is expensive, waitlists are months long, and sometimes you just need someone thoughtful to help untangle what is happening right now.

"I cannot afford $200 an hour, but I cannot keep spinning like this."

In the grey zone

You are not in crisis, but you are not fine. You are carrying something heavy: burnout, ambivalence, quiet grief: that friends get tired of hearing about.

"I am high-functioning enough that no one asks, but tired enough that I feel it constantly."

The self-reflective reader

You have read the books, you know your attachment style, and you are frustrated that intellectual understanding has not translated into change.

"I know all the theory. I need help seeing where I am fooling myself."

The seeker

Drawn to Stoicism, Taoism, Buddhism, or existential philosophy, but looking for practical application to your actual daily relationships and work.

"I do not want quotes. I want to know how Marcus Aurelius would handle this email."

Someone who wants the truth

Tired of AI that sounds like an HR manual or an eager cheerleader. You want a conversation partner with enough spine to push back constructively.

"Stop telling me I am valid. Tell me what I am doing wrong."

Acute psychiatric emergencies Diagnosis or medical assessment Users seeking uncritical agreement

Proof

What a real conversation looks like.

A synthetic dialogue excerpt illustrating Satori's core dynamic: identifying intellectual understanding used as an emotional shield.

User

"I've been in therapy for two years and I still feel stuck. I don't know if I'm the problem or if something else is going on."

Satori

"Two years is long enough to stop feeling like the problem is just time. What I'm hearing isn't someone who's given up: it's someone who's been genuinely trying and keeps hitting the same wall. That wall is worth looking at more carefully than 'am I the problem.' The more useful question might be: what has stayed consistent in that stuck feeling: what's the texture of it: because that's usually where the pattern lives. When you say stuck, what does stuck actually feel like for you? Not the story about it. The feeling."

User

"Like I understand everything. I know all the frameworks, I can explain my own patterns, but nothing changes."

Satori

"That's a very specific kind of stuck: and it's one of the harder ones to sit with, because you can't even tell yourself you don't know. You do know. The gap isn't understanding. The gap is between insight and something living in you that hasn't moved. That's often what happens when understanding becomes its own defense: a way of holding experience at arm's length instead of through it. The question isn't what you know. It's what you're still not letting yourself feel."

Bring the thing you have explained a dozen times and never felt heard on. That is the test.

How a conversation moves. Satori listens first, finds the real tension underneath the presenting one, offers a tentative reading you can correct, brings in one framework only when it sharpens things, and always ends with movement: a clearer frame, a smaller next step, or a physical experiment. The mechanics stay invisible on purpose.
Modular sharded architecture. Satori v6.5 guarantees that Turn 1 loads under 8,000 bytes. Fifteen modular reference frameworks (all strictly under 24 KB) load progressively on demand. Details: Architecture Specification.
When the situation calls for something deeper. Dedicated protocols exist for grief and ambiguous loss, for non-suicidal 3am existential despair (Dark Night mode), for Jungian shadow work, and for relationship conflict. Details: Specialized Protocols.
Data Sovereignty

Your words stay yours.

Satori stores short structural summaries (≤500 characters)—never raw transcripts—in a private local vault on your machine (~/.satori/memory/). It utilizes local SQLite FTS5 search with POSIX 0700 directory security and automatically blocks cloud-synced folders.

Right to Erasure: You can inspect your records, tell Satori to forget a specific theme, or purge the entire vault with SQLite VACUUM zero-slack erasure.

Per-Surface Privacy Distinction: Satori transmits zero product telemetry. However, if you talk to Satori on Claude.ai, ChatGPT, or Gemini Web, your conversation is processed by Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google under their respective terms. For 100% local privacy, run Satori in your terminal with a local open-weights model via Hermes Agent or OpenCode.

Questions

Common questions.

Is this actually free? What's the catch?

Free and open source under Apache 2.0. No subscription, no paid tier, no telemetry, no tracking, and zero advertising spend across the project life. Built by one builder who wanted a companion with actual depth and spine.

Is Satori a replacement for therapy?

No. Satori is not a therapist, does not provide clinical care, and does not diagnose mental health conditions. If you are dealing with clinical depression, trauma, severe anxiety, or psychiatric conditions, please work with a licensed human professional. Satori is for the territory between "I'm fine" and "I need clinical care": personal reflection, navigating difficult decisions, processing emotions, and exploring inner patterns. It works alongside therapy.

How does this compare to ChatGPT or other AI companions?

Most conversational AI acts as an eager assistant that validates everything you say, offers rapid-fire bulleted lists, or acts like an eager customer service representative. Satori is psychologically grounded, speaks in plain human language without therapy speak, holds its hypotheses lightly, and commits to movement rather than endless validation. It uses one framework at a time, never stacks jargon, and never talks down to you.

Does it remember me across conversations, and how is my privacy protected?

Satori remembers recurring themes across sessions using a private local vault on your machine (~/.satori/memory/), not raw transcripts. You can inspect your records, tell Satori to forget any topic, run off-the-record sessions, or purge the vault entirely. On web chat, Satori can export compact, portable Continuity Notes (≤600 chars) that you carry between sessions.

What if I'm in crisis?

Satori is not equipped for acute psychiatric emergencies or crisis intervention. In acute danger or self-harm, Satori immediately halts reflection and provides verified primary-source emergency lines:

  • United States (911): Call or text 988 (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) · Text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).
  • Canada (911): Call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline) · Text CONNECT to 686868.
  • United Kingdom (999): Call 111 (NHS Mental Health option) · Call 116 123 (Samaritans) · Text SHOUT to 85258.
  • Ireland (999 / 112): Call 116 123 (Samaritans Ireland) · Text HELLO to 50808.
  • Australia (000): Call 13 11 14 or text 0477 13 11 14 (Lifeline Australia).
  • New Zealand (111): Call or text 1737 (Need to Talk? 24/7).
  • India (112): Call 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (Tele-MANAS 24/7).
  • European Union: Call 112 (Emergency) or 116 123 (Emotional Support Helpline).
  • Global Directories: findahelpline.com (130+ countries) · IASP Crisis Centres.
Which AI agents and models does Satori support?

Satori supports 16 environments across 2 categories (10 Agent Harnesses including Claude Code, Antigravity, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Codex, Grok Build, Windsurf, OpenCode, Hermes Agent, OpenClaw; and 6 Web Chat Surfaces including Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini Web, Grok Web, DeepSeek, Meta AI). Rather than brittle model-name tables, Satori dynamically calibrates behavior across 5 durable Behavioral Profiles (October 2026 checkpoints):

  • Profile 1 (Recursive & STEM Reasoners / Extended CoT): e.g., Claude Fable 5.1, Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-6 Astra, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, o4-mini, Grok 4.3 (Reasoning). Enforces CoT emotional bypassing gates and single-hypothesis pruning in <think>.
  • Profile 2 (Literary & Constitutional Frontier): e.g., Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, Llama 5 (405B), Mistral Large 3. Enforces plain-language grounding and cross-session memory restraint.
  • Profile 3 (High-Candor & Search-Native): e.g., Grok 4.7, Grok 4.3, Perplexity Sonar Pro (2026). Suppresses uninvited web search during introspection and wraps direct pattern challenges in tactical empathy.
  • Profile 4 (High-Velocity Coding & RAG Workhorses): e.g., Claude Sonnet 5.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, GPT-6.1 Sol / Codex, Llama 5 (70B), Qwen 3.5 72B Coder. Enforces strict prose-only lock (no bullet lists or action plans) and protects technical tasks during developer burnout.
  • Profile 5 (Compact Edge <32B, Local & Voice): e.g., Claude Haiku 5.5, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, GPT-6 Luna / Nano, DeepSeek-V4-Lite, Grok Voice 3.0, Llama 5 8B / 14B (Local), Mistral Ministral 8B, Qwen 3.5 14B / 32B (Local). Enforces <12 KB context ceiling, single-file spine (references/core-compact.md), and question discipline.
Why a 95% Non-Activation Rate target instead of 100%?

Because prompt routing in LLM harnesses is probabilistic. We test against 200 hard coding cases and publish the exact results and confidence intervals. Claiming "100% zero hijack" would be technically dishonest.

Can I contribute or modify it?
