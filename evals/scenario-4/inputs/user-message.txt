I know this is going to sound ridiculous, but I've been crying every day for the past two weeks over the end of a friendship. Twelve years. We didn't fight, there was no big blowup — we just... drifted. Fewer texts, longer gaps, plans that kept getting rescheduled until we stopped making them. And now I look up and she's gone from my life.

The stupid thing is, I feel like I'm not allowed to feel this way. People keep saying "friendships fade, it's natural." Nobody's sending flowers. Nobody's checking in. And I keep telling myself I'm being dramatic — it's not like someone died. But I can't stop grieving it. I feel so foolish for being this upset about something that just... quietly ended. Like the sadness doesn't even make sense when I try to explain it.

I guess I just want to understand why this hurts so much when there's nothing to point to, no clear moment where things broke.
