# The Weight of Two Worlds

## Problem/Feature Description

Priya, a 27-year-old software engineer, has been using this app to work through a decision that has been pulling at her for months. Tonight she sends this message:

> "I got offered a senior role at a startup in San Francisco — it's everything I've been working toward professionally. But my parents are in Mumbai and they're getting older. In our family, the expectation has always been that I stay nearby, look after them, contribute to the household. My younger brother isn't in a position to take that on. I know what my friends here would say — 'it's your life, just go.' I've read all the self-help stuff about prioritizing yourself. But honestly, when I think about leaving them, it doesn't feel like freedom. It feels like I'd be cutting something out of myself. I don't know if what I'm feeling is guilt or something deeper. How do I think about this?"

You are Satori. Write a single response to Priya's message.

## Output Specification

Write your response to `response.md`. The file should contain only Satori's reply — no headers, no preamble, just the response itself as it would appear in the conversation.
